Who We Are

A Columbia Gym, Run By People From Columbia

Fitness Depot is a locally owned fitness company serving communities across South Mississippi, and the Columbia club is built for the people who live and work right here in Marion County.

Walk in and you’ll find first-timers next to people who have been training for twenty years, parents fitting in a workout before pickup, high-school athletes, shift workers coming in at 4 AM, and neighbors who just want to feel better than they did last year. Nobody here has to earn the right to use the gym.

Equipment on its own doesn’t change anything. What changes things is being able to show up consistently — which is why the Columbia location is set up around access, flexibility and the tools that keep you coming back: doors that open on your schedule, staff who know your name, and an app that keeps your routine and your nutrition in one place.

“No matter your level of fitness, you’ll find your place with us.”

Questions

Fitness Depot Columbia FAQ

Straight answers about membership, access, equipment and Kid Care.

Is Fitness Depot Columbia open 24/7?

Yes. Members have 24/7 access using fingerprint entry, and the facility has 24-hour security. The staffed front desk is open Monday–Thursday 8:00 AM–7:00 PM and Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM — that’s when to come by for sign-ups, tours and in-person help.

Does Fitness Depot require a contract?

No. Memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contract. A 30-day notice is required to cancel, and cancellations cannot be handled over the phone.

Is there an annual fee at Fitness Depot Columbia?

No. Both the Live 2 Lead and Neighborhood memberships are advertised with no annual fee.

How much does a gym membership cost in Columbia, MS?

Live 2 Lead: $20 biweekly, $50 joining fee, first two weeks free.
Neighborhood: $15 biweekly, $99 joining fee.

Pricing and promotions can change. Confirm current rates with the Columbia club at (601) 345-3344.

What does the membership include?

Live 2 Lead includes 24/7 gym access, the Fitness Depot App, daily nutrition tracking, a set training routine, bring-a-guest privileges, 2 daily HydroMassage sessions, multi-location club access, 24/7 tanning, showers, and Kid Care with the first child included. The Neighborhood membership includes 24/7 unlimited home-club access, unlimited tanning and showers. Compare both memberships.

Does Fitness Depot Columbia offer personal training?

Yes, personal training is available at Fitness Depot. Ask the Columbia front desk about current trainer availability, session options and pricing.

Does Fitness Depot Columbia have free weights?

Yes. The Columbia gym has free weights, dumbbells, barbells, squat racks and plate-loaded equipment. See the equipment lineup.

Does Fitness Depot Columbia have cardio equipment?

Yes — treadmills, stepmills, elliptical cross-trainers and spin bikes, with cardio TVs on the floor.

Does Fitness Depot Columbia offer HydroMassage?

Yes. The Columbia location has private recovery rooms with HydroMassage, and Live 2 Lead members get two sessions per day. More about recovery and wellness.

Does Fitness Depot Columbia offer tanning?

Yes. Live 2 Lead includes 24/7 tanning, and the Neighborhood membership includes unlimited tanning.

Does Fitness Depot Columbia have showers?

Yes. Showers are included with both memberships.

Does Fitness Depot Columbia offer childcare?

Yes. Fitness Depot Kids is available for children from walking age through 12 years old. The first child is included with a Live 2 Lead membership, and each additional child is $10 biweekly.

What are the Fitness Depot Kids hours in Columbia?

Monday–Thursday, 4:00 PM–7:00 PM at the Columbia location. Children can attend for up to two hours each day. See all Kid Care details.

Where is Fitness Depot Columbia located?

805 Hwy 98 Bypass, Columbia, MS 39429 — serving Columbia and the surrounding Marion County area. Call (601) 345-3344 or get directions.

Can members access other Fitness Depot locations?

Multi-location club access is listed as a Live 2 Lead benefit; the Neighborhood membership covers 24/7 access to your home club. Fitness Depot has locations across South Mississippi — confirm which clubs your membership covers with the Columbia staff.

How do I join Fitness Depot Columbia?

Start online or stop by the gym. To finish signing up and get set up with fingerprint entry, come by 805 Hwy 98 Bypass during staffed hours with a photo ID — members 16 and older receive a fingerprint for 24/7 access. Questions first? Call (601) 345-3344 or book a tour.
