The moment that matters

Almost nobody joins a gym on impulse while driving past. They decide first, then they look — on a phone, usually at night, usually searching something like "gym in Columbia MS."

Whatever they find in that moment does the selling. If it answers their questions fast, they walk in. If it makes them work for it — hunting for hours, pricing, whether there's childcare — they close the tab and check the next place. That search result is the front door, and it's open at 11pm when the front desk isn't.

The site has one job: get them through the door.

Every decision below was made against that, not against what looks impressive in a mockup.

The path from search to signup

The page is ordered the way a decision actually gets made — each section answering the objection the last one raises.

  1. They landThe headline confirms they're in the right place: Columbia, 24/7, every level.
  2. They understand youA locally owned gym for South Mississippi — not a chain, not intimidating.
  3. They see the priceBoth memberships laid out plainly. No contract and no annual fee are the first things they read, because those are the two things people brace for.
  4. They picture themselves thereEquipment by category, the turf area, recovery rooms, Kid Care hours — so a parent, a lifter and a first-timer each find their own reason.
  5. They trust itReal photos of the real building, honest hours, a straight answer to sixteen common questions.
  6. They actJoin Now stays visible the whole way down — pinned to the top on desktop, to the bottom bar on a phone.

Built to be found in Columbia

Looking good is table stakes. Being found is what fills the floor. The site is built for local search specifically — not generic "SEO."

  • Machine-readable business details. Structured data tells Google your address, phone, hours, amenities and both membership prices directly — the information that feeds map results and the business panel.
  • Staffed hours vs 24/7 access, kept separate. A 24-hour gym listed with 8–7 hours loses the late-night searcher. The site draws that distinction in the copy, in the structured data, and in a live "front desk open now" indicator.
  • Sixteen FAQs marked up for search. Answers can surface directly on the results page, which is where "does the gym in Columbia have childcare" gets settled.
  • Location worked into the writing, not stuffed into it. Columbia and Marion County read naturally in the headings and image descriptions. Keyword-stuffed pages get filtered; pages that sound like a person get read.

Built for the phone in their hand

Someone searching for a gym at night is on a phone. The site was designed at phone width first and checked at every size, not shrunk down afterwards.

  • A call button that's always there. Join Now and tap-to-call sit in a fixed bar at the bottom of the screen the entire time they scroll.
  • Nothing to pinch or drag sideways. Verified at 390px, 820px and 1440px — no horizontal scrolling anywhere.
  • Fast on a rural signal. Plain HTML with no page builder and no heavy framework, so it loads on a weak connection instead of spinning.
  • Readable for everyone. Text contrast, keyboard navigation and screen-reader labels were built in — including the gym's gold, which needs black text on it to stay legible.

What I need from you

  • Photographs of the Columbia gym. The single biggest thing standing between this and finished.
