# Brand assets — what to replace

The **colour palette is now the real one**, taken from the official Fitness Depot logo
(gold and black). The **logo artwork itself is still a placeholder** — the logo was
supplied as an image in conversation rather than as a file, and `fdgyms.com` is blocked by
the build environment's network policy, so the artwork file could not be written into the
repo. Dropping it in is a two-minute job (below).

## 1. The logo

The header and footer currently use an interim CSS/text lockup: a gold `FD` monogram box
plus *FITNESS* in white and *DEPOT* in gold — a nod to the logo's two-tone treatment, not a
reproduction of it. The barbell-squat mascot and the arched lettering are **not** recreated;
they should come from the official artwork.

**To use the official logo**, replace the markup in `index.html` — it appears twice, in
`<header class="header">` and in `<footer class="footer">`, both marked with a comment:

```html
<a class="logo" href="#top" aria-label="Fitness Depot Columbia — home">
  <img src="assets/img/logo-fitness-depot.svg"
       alt="Fitness Depot Columbia"
       width="200" height="44">
</a>
```

Prefer an **SVG**. The header and footer are both near-black, so a single logo that works
on a dark ground covers both — the full-colour logo on white will need a version with the
lettering knocked out to white, or a light plate behind it.
The `.logo__loc` line ("COLUMBIA, MS") is worth keeping under a wordmark — it reinforces
the location on every screen.

`assets/favicon/favicon.svg` and `assets/favicon/apple-touch-icon.png` are also
placeholders built from the same monogram. Replace them with the real mark.

## 2. The colour palette — matched to the logo

Gold and black, sampled from the official logo. All brand values live at the top of
`assets/css/styles.css`:

```css
:root {
  --fd-gold:       #c9a227;  /* brand gold — fills, borders, accents on dark */
  --fd-gold-deep:  #a8851c;  /* large gold text on light; secondary fills */
  --fd-gold-text:  #8a6d12;  /* small gold text on light backgrounds */
  --fd-gold-light: #e0bc4a;  /* button hover; highlights on dark */
  --fd-gold-glow:  rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.32);
  --fd-ink:        #0d0d0d;  /* brand black — primary dark surface */
  --fd-ink-2:      #161616;  /* raised dark surface (cards on dark) */
  --fd-ink-3:      #232323;  /* borders/edges on dark */
}
```

### Why there are three golds

This is the one rule to keep if you adjust anything. Gold is a mid-tone, so it behaves
very differently depending on what is behind it:

| Combination | Contrast | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Gold on black | **8.0:1** | Excellent — use freely |
| Black text on a gold button | **8.0:1** | Excellent — this is why buttons use black text |
| White text on a gold button | 2.4:1 | **Fails** — never do this |
| Gold text on white | 2.4:1 | **Fails** — use `--fd-gold-text` (4.9:1) instead |
| `--fd-gold-deep` on white | 3.5:1 | Large/bold text and icons only |

So: **gold buttons always take black text**, and gold used as text on a light background
steps down to `--fd-gold-deep` (large) or `--fd-gold-text` (small). The stylesheet already
handles this — the eyebrow labels, checkmarks, stat numerals and FAQ chevrons each pick the
right shade for the surface they sit on.

The header and top bar are **black** rather than white. That is deliberate: it matches the
logo's own ground and lets the gold run at full strength across the most important part of
the page instead of being forced into a muted shade.

The placeholder images embed the same gold and black and are regenerated by
`tools/make-placeholders.sh`; they disappear as real photos arrive.

## 3. Typography

Headings use **Barlow Condensed** (bold, condensed, uppercase) and body copy uses
**Inter**, both loaded from Google Fonts in the `<head>`. They were chosen to read as
strong and modern without being a novelty gym font.

If Fitness Depot has an official typeface, replace the two font URLs and update
`--display` / `--body` in `:root`. Keep a real fallback stack: the display fallback is
`'Arial Narrow', Arial` — a condensed fallback matters, because a wide fallback changes
how much space the headlines take.
